# CLAUSE 5 LEADERSHIP

 ## 5.1 Leadership and Commitment
> ** The business owner demonstrates the ISMS leadership by Implementing security checks into daily real estate workflow ( Checking the file storage hygiene before closing the office and checking if the devices screens are locked or shutdown)

# As the solo business owner acting as top management, leadership and commitment are demonstrated by:
1. **Policy:**  Never expose unmasked client KYC data.
2. **Objectives:** Lock your screen every time you step away and zero surprise data lockouts or ransomware.
3. **Process Integration:** Do a quick file storage cleanup during end-of-day office lockup and no separate compliance paperwork
4. **Resource Allocation:** 15 minutes a month on OS/app updates and backup checks, keep full-disk encryption on and use a privacy screen filter if needed.
5. **Communication:** Clear  and transparent set expectations with clients on how their data is handled.
6. **Expected Results:** Protect client trust, avoid local compliance friction, and keep operations running smoothly.
7. **Directing & Supporting:** Maintaining personal operational discipline and using quick habits (e.g., shortcut keys to lock workstation before stepping away for a coffee or walk-in client).
8. **Continual Improvement:** Adjusting security habits ( tightening WhatsApp file sharing habits or updating portal passwords) when noticing new operational risks or platform changes.
9. **Multi-Role Accountability:** Applying these standards uniformly across all operations worn by the owner (client acquisition, document custodian, and IT self-support)

## 5.2 Information Security Policy

> **Project RealitySec:** is committed to safeguarding client personal information (PAN, phone numbers, agreement hard copies) and transactional data against accidental disclosure, physical walk in visibility, or unauthorized device access.

### Policy Operational Alignment
• **Purpose of the organization :** Tailored to solo real estate brokerage operations in urban Bangalore,
• **Information security Objectives:** Zero leaks of unmasked client ID/KYC info, paired with tight physical and digital lockups.
• **Applicable  Requirements :** Follows standard data protection rules for private personal and financial details.
• **Continual Improvement:** Checked once a year, or whenever we switch to new listing websites or chat/email tools.
• **Document Control & Availability:** Maintained as documented ISMS information within the project repository; communicated transparently to clients and made available to relevant interested parties upon verified request.
• **Communication:** Clear on boarding rules tell clients how their ID documents are kept safe, and the team follows good daily habits.
• **Availability for Interested Parties:** Saved inside ISMS project docs and shared upon request with enterprise clients, partners, or auditors who need to verify it.

## Clause 5.3 Organizational Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities
> **Project RealtySec** is a solo-operator practice, all information security responsibilities are assigned across the functional "hats" worn by the proprietor:
  
**1. Principal Broker** *(Sales & Client Intake)* 
• Only ask for ID or financial papers when strictly needed
• Encourage clients to send masked IDs or use secure channels
• Stay inside safe data boundaries on property listing websites.
• Can pause or walk away from a deal if someone insists on unsafe data practices.

**2. Document Custodian** 
• Keep physical paper agreements locked away safely.
• Clear out temporary digital files and clean up folders at the end of the day.
• Delete outdated client draft files and lock down folder access.

**3. IT & Security Self-Admin** 
• Keep laptop disk encryption (FileVault/BitLocker) turned on.
• Spend 15 minutes a month checking updates and backups.
• Build the habit of locking your screen instantly when walking away.
• Can force logouts, change portal passwords, or lock down devices.

**4. Top Management** *(Governance / Owner)*
• Check your security setup once a year or whenever your workflow changes.
• Confirm that monthly backup checks actually took place.
• The only person who can change rules, accept risks, or update policies.

### Accountability & Reporting
• As top management, the proprietor self-audits adherence to these baseline habits during monthly 15-minute reviews.
• Client facing terms or on boarding notes implicitly communicate that the broker holds authority over data hygiene standards during transactions.
