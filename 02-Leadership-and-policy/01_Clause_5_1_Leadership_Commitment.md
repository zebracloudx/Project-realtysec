## 1. Executive Commitment Statement
The Managing Broker and top management of **RealtySec India Pvt Ltd** hereby commit to establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS) tailored to our real estate operations in Bangalore. 

We recognize that safeguarding client Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as Aadhaar, PAN details, and financial records is fundamental to maintaining client trust and complying with regional data privacy laws like the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

---

## 2. Core Leadership Responsibilities (ISO 27001 Clause 5.1 Alignment)
To demonstrate active leadership and commitment, top management pledges to:

1. **Integration with Business Objectives:** Ensure that information security requirements are integrated into RealtySec’s daily property deal workflows, client onboarding, and digital interactions.
2. **Resource Allocation:** Provide adequate resources—including secure cloud tools, Mobile Device Management (MDM) guidelines, and staff security awareness training—necessary for the ISMS.
3. **Communication & Awareness:** Communicate the importance of effective information security management and of conforming to ISMS requirements to all team members (field agents and brokers).
4. **Continuous Improvement:** Ensure that the ISMS achieves its intended outcomes through regular reviews, risk assessments, and audit oversight.
5. **Individual Accountability:** Enforce strict access control standards, eliminating shared account practices and ensuring clear accountability across all 4 endpoints.
