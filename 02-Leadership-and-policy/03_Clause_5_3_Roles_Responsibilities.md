## 1. Purpose & Organizational Context
To ensure the effective operation of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) at **RealtySec India Pvt Ltd**, top management has assigned specific security roles, responsibilities, and reporting authorities. Given our lean structure (1 Managing Broker + 3 Field Agents), accountability is clearly delineated to ensure no security duty is overlooked.

---

## 2. Security Roles & Responsibility Matrix (RACI Model)

| Role / Title | Key ISMS Responsibilities & Authorities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Management / Managing Broker** | • Approves ISMS policies, scope, and risk appetite.<br>• Allocates necessary resources and budget.<br>• Conducts management reviews. |
| **Lead Auditor / GRC Lead (Sreerekh)** | • Maintains and updates ISMS documentation on GitHub.<br>• Conducts internal audits and risk assessments.<br>• Reports compliance status directly to leadership. |
| **Field Agents (Team of 3)** | • Adhering to Acceptable Use, BYOD, and password policies.<br>• Reporting lost devices, phishing, or security incidents immediately.<br>• Ensuring client KYC files are handled securely via official cloud tools. |

---

## 3. Authority for Reporting Non-Conformities
* All employees and field agents have the authority and obligation to report any security weakness, suspected data leak, or policy violation directly to the Managing Broker or GRC Lead without fear of reprisal.
* The GRC Lead is granted full organizational authority to investigate security incidents and recommend corrective actions.
