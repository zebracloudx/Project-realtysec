## 1. Purpose & Objective
The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to establish the overarching security rules and behavioral expectations for all personnel (Managing Broker and Field Agents) at **RealtySec India Pvt Ltd**. As a cloud-native real estate firm operating in Bangalore, protecting client confidentiality, digital assets, and Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is vital to our legal obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and our client trust commitments.

---

## 2. Scope & Applicability
This policy applies to:
* All 4 team members of RealtySec (1 Managing Broker + 3 Field Agents).
* All company-related data assets, including customer KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN), property agreements, and financial ledgers.
* All endpoints and communication channels utilized for business operations, including personal mobile devices (BYOD) and Google Workspace tenant environments.

---

## 3. Key Security Directives (Policy Principles)

### 3.1 Access Control & Authentication
* **No Shared Credentials:** Every staff member must use a unique, individual login for Google Workspace and company tools. Shared accounts are strictly prohibited.
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** MFA must be enabled on all accounts accessing company cloud storage and communication portals.

### 3.2 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) & Endpoint Security
* **Device Protection:** All personal phones and laptops used for RealtySec business must have screen-lock passcodes enabled, auto-lock timers configured, and full-disk encryption active where supported.
* **Secure Messaging:** Business-related client communications and document transfers over WhatsApp or mobile apps must be handled securely, ensuring sensitive files are transferred only to official cloud repositories rather than left unprotected on local storage.

### 3.3 Data Protection & Privacy
* **Data Minimization:** Only collect client information necessary for property transactions, ensuring compliance with DPDP data privacy requirements.
* **Confidentiality:** Client PII and property records must never be shared with unauthorized third parties or exposed in public environments (e.g., unsecured cafe Wi-Fi without VPN precautions).

### 3.4 Incident Reporting
* Any lost device, suspected phishing attempt, or accidental data exposure must be reported immediately to the Managing Broker so swift mitigation steps can be taken.

---

## 4. Enforcement & Compliance
Failure to adhere to this Information Security Policy may result in a review of system access privileges, disciplinary action, or termination of association with RealtySec. This policy is reviewed annually by top management to ensure its continued suitability.
