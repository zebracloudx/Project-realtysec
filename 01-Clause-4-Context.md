# Clause 4: Context of the Organization

# 4.1 Understanding the Organization and its Context
In order to establish ISMS to a hypothetical real estate agency, We must determine the external and internal issue that are relevant to its purpose and that affect its ability to achieve the intended outcome.

### External Issues 

- Client expectations regarding data privacy (PAN numbers, bank details).
- Dependence on third party property portals (MagicBricks or 99acres) and communication tools (WhatsApp) whose security and servers are entirely managed by outside companies.
### Internal Issues
- Solo operator working out of a local shop.
- Dependence on a single personal laptop and smartphone.
- Physical office security where walk-in strangers or Clients see sensitive client documents or phone numbers left lying on the desk.
  

| Sr.No. | Issue Type | Issue Description | Stakeholder/Interested Parties | Impact | Risk | Coverage in ISMS Implementation
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | External | Local real estate market regulatory compliance | Real Estate Regulatory Authorities | Fines, legal liability, or loss of license due to non-compliance in handling transaction records | High | Legal & Regulatory Compliance Policy, Information Classification Policy |


# 4.2 Understanding the needs and expectation of the interested parties
we need to identify the stakeholders relevant to our ISMS and what requirements do they have from us

## Key interested parties and their expectations
- Clients (Property Buyers and Sellers) -strict privacy of their personal information (PAN card, Aadhar Card, Bank details, Phone number, Property Tax ID's)
- Real estate regulatory authorities- Following legal protocols, proper handling of property transaction records) 
- The Business owners - Make sure his device don't break and deals aren't lost and protection against financial fraud or data theft

# 4.3 Determining the Scope of ISMS
The scope of the ISMS covers functionality of the solo real estate agency, specifically focusing on the digital and physical handling of client property records, communication channel and transaction data managed by a single laptop and smartphone from a local shop in bengaluru

## Determining In Scope
- Internal Issue - Operator managing the transaction, handling documents and the device single-handedly
- External Issue- Establishing boundaries against the third party platforms, Our ISMS States that third party and messaging servers are Out of scope.
Their infrastructure is managed by external vendors and not the solo office.

# INTERFACES
- client inquires back and forth, sending Transactional and property documents via whatsapp and email, property ads posted in public domains (magicbricks and 99 acres)
# DEPARTMENT 
- Self Managed by owner, no separate IT or HR departments, Proprietor handles their own device passwords and device security
